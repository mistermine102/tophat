About Tophat

A free chess coach that reviews your games move by move, in your browser.

Open one of your chess.com games or paste a game, and Tophat labels every move (best, good, inaccuracy, mistake, blunder …), explains why a move was bad and shows what would have been better. The analysis is done by the Stockfish chess engine, running inside your own browser tab.

Privacy

Tophat has no accounts and no database of its own. In short: your games stay on your device.

  • On your device: the games you open, their analyses and your settings (sound, board options) are saved in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB and localStorage), so a game you open again is ready at once. They are not sent to us. To delete them, clear this site's data in your browser's settings.
  • chess.com: when you look up a chess.com username, your browser asks chess.com's public API (api.chess.com) for that player's games. chess.com then receives your IP address and the username, as with any visit to their site; their own privacy policy applies. Tophat is not affiliated with chess.com.
  • Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, the page requested, browser type) to keep the site running and secure. They are not used for anything else and are deleted regularly.
  • No tracking: there are no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies. The fonts and the engine are served from this site, not from third parties.

If that changes (for example if ads are added), this page will say so first.
