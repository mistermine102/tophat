# Explanation review loop (M5 prep)

Games: the Opera game (Morphy 1858) and 6 real Lichess games (~1500–1600): PuBQ0CoQ (Caro-Kann Panov),
lmnbpO0B (Pirc), 5frUvyHl (Scandinavian), mcz8Z7Cr (Scotch Gambit), dmVsbMbS (Ruy Lopez Steinitz), tM14SUiA
(Semi-Slav); plus the regression fragment `bench/games/blackburne-shilling-nxe5.pgn` (4.Nxe5? Qg5).
Engine: Stockfish 19, 5 threads, default budget (deep d18 / 5 s cap). Searches cached between rounds, so every
round re-renders the same engine lines.

Per game reviewed: every Inaccuracy / Mistake / Blunder / Miss / Great move plus 15 pseudo-random other moves
(fixed seed), every sentence of the played move's three levels, the label text, the verdict, and the suggestion
shown (Try this / Engine's top). Review sheets are generated by `tmp/review-dump.mjs` from the analyze-pgn JSON;
claims were checked on the board with chessops (`tmp/board.ts`: board after SAN moves, attackers, SEE).

Categories: **FALSE** (wrong on the board / contradicts the line), **MISLEADING** (true but wrong impression),
**USELESS** (empty level / sentence that says nothing, or a missing reason where facts exist), **WEAK** (wording).
